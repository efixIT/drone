Portal Guide & Technical Specifications

Mouse Controls

  • Left Click + Drag: Orbit / Rotate view
  • Right Click + Drag: Pan / Move camera
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom in / Zoom out

Touch Controls

  • One Finger Drag: Orbit / Move camera
  • Two Finger Drag: Pan view
  • Pinch Gesture: Zoom in / Zoom out

Survey Metadata

  • Deployment Platform: DJI Matrice 300 RTK
  • Sensor Payload: Zenmuse L2 LiDAR & RGB
  • Correction Base: D-RTK 3 GNSS Mobile Station
  • Target Precision: Horizontal: 1-3cm | Vertical: 2-5cm
System Compatibility: The underlying 3D Potree environment is natively optimized for desktop architectures. While touch screens are fully supported for basic viewing, spatial rotation and high-precision orbit tracking can occasionally feel less fluid on mobile or tablet browsers. For the absolute best user experience, a standard mouse setup is highly recommended.
